## Flat Mode vs Spatial Smoked Glass

- **Vision Loft / Obsidian / Aurora**: Layered `backdrop-filter: blur(42px)` glassmorphism panels over a soft architectural room scene or custom user wallpaper.
- **Flat Dark & Flat Light**: Disables backdrop compositing filters (`backdrop-filter: none`) for lower GPU usage, maximum crispness on low-power devices, and classic clean documentation contrast.

Need custom background photography? Click the **`+`** icon in the floating top address bar or the **gear** icon on the left vertical dock to open the **Theme Studio Drawer** from any page.
